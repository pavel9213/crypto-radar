# Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Crypto Radar to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Share reproducible examples and relevant context.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's credentials or personal information.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub.
