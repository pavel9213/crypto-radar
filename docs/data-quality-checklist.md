# Generated feed data quality checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing a new collector run.

- Confirm each item has a stable ID, project, title, category, status, date, and source URL.
- Spot-check that source URLs open the cited publication rather than an unrelated homepage.
- Confirm publication dates are plausible and timezone-aware timestamps are normalized to UTC.
- Check whether a project update was incorrectly categorized as TGE, Snapshot, Airdrop, Points, or Partnership.
- Treat repeated aggregator headlines as possible duplicates, even when their URLs differ.
- Keep speculative language visible; do not promote an unconfirmed claim because it is widely repeated.
- Compare the feed item count with recent runs and investigate unusually large changes.

Passing the schema validator confirms structure, not editorial accuracy.
