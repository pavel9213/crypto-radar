# Glossary

- **Airdrop** — distribution of tokens or assets to eligible users.
- **Confirmed** — an item originated from a configured primary source; not a guarantee of independent verification.
- **Points** — campaign points, XP, or leaderboard activity that may or may not convert to a token allocation.
- **Primary source** — a project's own verified website, documentation, or official account.
- **Rumor** — a claim with speculation, leak, or uncertainty language.
- **Snapshot** — a recorded state of accounts or activity used to determine eligibility at a particular time.
- **TGE** — token generation event, commonly used for the initial creation or launch of a token.
- **Unconfirmed** — an item from secondary coverage that has not been confirmed by a primary source.
- **X handle** — an account name on X, without the leading @ when used in configuration.
