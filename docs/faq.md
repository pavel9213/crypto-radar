# FAQ

## Does Confirmed mean the claim is true?

No. It indicates that an item came from a configured official source. A project can still publish a plan, estimate, or mistaken statement.

## Does points activity guarantee an airdrop?

No. Crypto Radar tracks public signals and does not guarantee allocations.

## Why is a project missing from the feed?

It may not publish RSS/Atom, its homepage may not expose a feed, or a source may be temporarily unavailable. Optional X monitoring also depends on API access.

## Why do duplicates or irrelevant items appear?

Aggregators and keyword-based classification can produce duplicates or false positives. Review the source link and report reproducible cases.

## Where are manual entries stored?

In the browser profile's local storage unless exported; they are not automatically synchronized.
