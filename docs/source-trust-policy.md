# Source trust policy

Crypto Radar separates where a claim came from from whether the claim is true.

## Labels

- **Confirmed**: published by a configured official project source. This is source attribution, not independent fact-checking.
- **Unconfirmed**: secondary coverage or a claim without authoritative confirmation.
- **Rumor**: text explicitly signals speculation, leaks, or an unverified claim.
- **Monitoring**: an item worth tracking whose meaning or status is not yet clear.

## Review rules

1. Prefer a project's own website, documentation, or verified social account.
2. Link to the original publisher whenever possible.
3. Never promote a claim to Confirmed solely because multiple aggregators repeat it.
4. Preserve uncertainty in titles and notes rather than rewriting speculation as fact.
5. Re-check token launch dates, snapshot claims, and eligibility rules before acting on them.

This policy is editorial guidance, not financial advice.
