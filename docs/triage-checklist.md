# Signal triage checklist

Before treating an item as actionable, check:

- [ ] Does the source URL open and match the headline?
- [ ] Is the publisher the project's official website or account?
- [ ] Is the announcement explicit, rather than a hint, meme, or ambiguous reply?
- [ ] Does the source give a specific date, timezone, eligibility rule, or contract address?
- [ ] Could the item refer to a different project with a similar name?
- [ ] Is the claim contradicted or corrected by a later official post?
- [ ] If only secondary outlets report it, is it still labeled Unconfirmed?
- [ ] If the language indicates speculation or a leak, is it labeled Rumor?

Never connect a wallet, sign a transaction, or share a seed phrase because a news item claims an airdrop is available. Open official sources independently and check domains carefully.
